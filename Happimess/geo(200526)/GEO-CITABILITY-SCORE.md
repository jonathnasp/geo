## Note on Homepage vs. Inner Pages

The homepage citability score of **29/100** does not mean the site is uncitable overall. It means AI systems will preferentially cite inner pages:

- An AI answering "What makes Happimess special?" will cite **About Us** (testing methodology block: 80/100)
- An AI answering "what size dual trash can should I buy?" will cite **the blog guide** (recommendation block: 77/100)
- An AI answering "how do I contact Happimess?" will cite the **footer** on the homepage (72/100)

The homepage's job in GEO is different from inner pages: it should **establish entity identity** (what is this brand, where is it, what does it make) so AI systems can confidently ground all other citations to the Happimess entity. Currently it fails this job because there's no prose description, no founding story, no unique differentiation copy.

**Recommended homepage GEO strategy:** Add 3 short prose blocks (brand description, testing claim, price anchors) totaling ~200 words. This is enough to push the homepage from "cannot cite" to "confirms brand identity."
