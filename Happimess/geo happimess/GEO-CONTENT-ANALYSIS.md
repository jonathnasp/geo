## Dimension 1: Experience — 8/25

Experience (the first E in E-E-A-T) asks: *does the content demonstrate that the author has actually used, tested, or lived with the thing they're writing about?*

### What's Missing

**No original product testing data published.** The About-Us page documents a genuine quality process — 30-day real-world evaluation and 500+ cycle mechanism durability testing. This is real experience. Yet no blog post has ever published a finding from these tests. The content reads as if written from a product catalog, not from someone who spent 30 days using the product.

**No before/after comparisons.** A post about dual trash cans should include photos or descriptions of what the author's kitchen looked like with a single can vs. a dual can. None of the posts do this.

**No first-person narrative.** Every post is written in a detached, third-person advisory voice. "Families generate trash quickly" instead of "When we tested this with a household of four over three weeks, we found..."

**Anonymous customer testimonials.** The trash bag post includes customer quotes ("The lemon scent actually keeps my kitchen fresher between trash days") with no attribution — no name, no date, no platform link, no purchase verification. These are indistinguishable from fabricated testimonials in the eyes of both AI systems and human readers.

**Rupee symbol artifact.** The economy home decor post contains `₹2000 or $25` in a FAQ answer. This is a high-confidence AI-generation artifact indicating content was drafted by a tool trained on multinational datasets without editorial correction. An NYC-based brand writing from experience would not include Indian rupee pricing in a U.S.-targeted post.

### What Would Score Well (Target: 20+/25)

- A "30-day test" post documenting what was evaluated, how, and what was found (with named testers and photos)
- Real customer attribution: "Sarah T., Brooklyn, verified purchase, March 2026"
- Product specification data from actual physical testing: bag thickness in mils, mechanism pull-force in pounds, cycle count at point of wear
- Author introduction mentioning specific experience with the product category ("I've organized 50+ apartments across NYC")

**No third-party review platform badge.** No Trustpilot, Google Reviews, or Shopper Approved integration visible. These signals tell prospective buyers that a neutral third party is aggregating real purchase feedback.
